FAQs

1. How long does it take to close an OTC deal?

Typically, OTC deals take between 24 to 48 hours, depending on the project’s investment readiness, investor interest, and deal complexity.

2. What are the key factors for securing an exchange listing?
  • Market demand and trading volume potential.
  • Token utility and project credibility.
  • Compliance with exchange requirements.
3. How does 3Aru Labs ensure regulatory compliance?

We conduct KYC (Know Your Customer) and AML (Anti-Money Laundering) checks for all transactions, ensuring compliance with financial regulations.

4. What sets 3Aru Labs apart from other business development firms?
  • Strong investor network with direct access to high-value investors.
  • Hands-on business development tailored to each project.
  • Industry expertise with a track record of successful fundraising and partnerships.
5. Does 3Aru Labs provide post-listing market support?
